## 3. Blind title labels: dropped

Per the user (2026-11-25), titles follow the rules our earlier research already backs, so the title-only labelling of all 1,960 was dropped. Moment category and cause come from the video labels in section 4, which judge the clip itself.

## 4. Measured video sample (299)

- **Sample:** `clipper viral sample`, 150 high and 150 low, matched channel by channel (round-robin over the 25 channels, cached media first). 299 measured (one download failed): 149 high, 150 low.
- **Downloads:** one at a time, 6–9 s apart. Media lives in `viral/media/` and is purged after 30 days.
- **Measured with code:** length, words per second, cuts per minute, dead space, loudest jump, opening text at 0.3 s (Apple Vision: words, area, type), face share, layout, burned-in captions, flashes, audio bed, visual loop and spoken opening type.
- **Vision failures:** Apple Vision failed on 3 clips, and their Vision fields are dropped.
- **Blind labels:** 20 sub-agents, 15 clips each, labelled each clip from its contact sheet (0.3/1/2/3 s and the last frame) and timed transcript, outcome hidden. Fields: category, cause shown, opening, visual hook, text role, premise and payoff time, ending, streamers.
- **Files:** derived numbers in `research/100k-measures-2026-11.json`, labels in `research/100k-labels-2026-11.json`. No transcripts are kept.
- **Not measured:** zooms (frames can't show them) and music vs game sound (the audio bed can't tell them apart). The ending's mid-sentence cut is judged by the labels, because Apple's speech recognition punctuates every last word.

### No difference (dropped as rules)

- **Payoff time** (labels): 14.9 s vs 15.0 s. **Tail after the payoff:** 8.6 s vs 7.8 s.
- **Ending type:** reaction, punchline, mid-sentence or after.
- **Opening type:** action first, reaction first or talking.
- **Opening text** that adds context vs a mere label; its word count.
- **Burned-in captions** (66% vs 66%) and their style.
- **Flashes:** 48% vs 48%.
- **Face size at 0.3 s.**
- **Visual loop:** too rare to test.
- **Categories** consequence and misunderstanding.
- **Every title-form trait** in section 2.

### What this changes in the clipper

- **Moment selection:** categories are listed in lift order: live event, discovery, chemistry, crossover, misunderstanding, consequence, character behaviour, reward. Picks must show the cause, not just a reaction (`cause`, plus the lead-up block in `candidates.md`).
- **Checks** (`clipper check`, fail when clear and `principles: on`, note when lean or in shadow):
  - split layout when the stream has a cam;
  - at least one situation-label hook;
  - opening text present;
  - words per second at or under the 100K+ 75th percentile (3.09);
  - length at or over the 100K+ 25th percentile (18.3 s).
- **Render:** with `principles: on` the no-hook arm stops, because openings without text clearly do worse. Flashes, captions and their style show no difference, so their defaults stay.
- **Kept outside the profile:** the payoff-by-~16 s and end-within-5 s notes come from our own channel's results, not from this comparison, and stay as notes. Title rules stay as our research has them (user decision).
- **Decide before `principles: on`:** the length trait is clear but small (1.09×). With it, a clip under 18.3 s would fail, against our own channel's 12–25 s winners.
